🕐
提早打卡不等於可以提早開始工作 / Clocking In Early Does Not Mean You May Start Work Early
Employees are welcome to clock in early. However, beginning work before your scheduled start time requires supervisor and HR to be informed. If we are not notified, hours worked before the scheduled start time will be adjusted back to the scheduled start time. If you need to start early for any reason, please make sure to notify your supervisor and HR so it can be properly authorized and recorded. Violations may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination.
員工可以提前打卡，但提前開始工作必須通知主管及HR。若未收到通知，排班時間前的工時將被調整回正常排班時間。如有需要提前開始工作，請記得告知主管和HR，以便正式授權並記錄在案。違反此規定可能導致紀律處分，最高包括解僱。
🍽️
用餐時間規定 / Meal Break Policy
California employees: Under California law, all employees must take an uninterrupted, off-duty 30-minute meal break before completing 5 hours of work. This is a legal requirement.
New Jersey & Georgia employees: For a regular 8-hour shift, employees should take a 30-minute unpaid meal break. Please plan work schedules accordingly to ensure employees are fully relieved from duty during the meal break.

For all locations: Meal breaks must not be interrupted for any work-related reason. If your meal break is delayed or interrupted, you are required to notify HR. Any concerns regarding your timecard should also be directed to HR.
加州員工：依加州法律，所有員工必須在工作滿5小時前，完整使用30分鐘的非值班用餐時間，此為法律強制規定。
新澤西及喬治亞州員工：正常8小時班，員工應安排30分鐘不帶薪用餐休息。請依現場工作情況合理安排，確保員工在用餐期間完全停止工作。
所有地點適用：用餐時間不得因任何工作原因被打斷。如用餐時間遭到延誤或打斷，請立即通知HR。如有任何工時記錄疑問，亦請聯繫HR。
📋
工時記錄必須正確 / Timecard Accuracy is Required
All employees are required to ensure their timecard accurately reflects actual hours worked. If you notice any discrepancy, please contact HR promptly. Falsifying or tampering with time records is strictly prohibited and may result in disciplinary action, up to and including immediate termination.
所有員工必須確保打卡記錄如實反映實際工作時數。如發現任何出入，請盡快聯繫HR。偽造或竄改打卡記錄嚴格禁止，可能導致紀律處分，最高包括立即解僱。
⚠️ 提醒 / Please Note

These policies are in place to ensure that all employees are treated fairly and that the company remains compliant. If any of the above situations arise — including starting work early without approval or a meal break being interrupted — please report it to your supervisor and HR promptly.

以上規定的目的是確保所有員工受到公平對待，並維護公司的合規運作。如發生任何上述情況——包括未經批准提前開始工作或用餐時間被打斷——請及時告知主管及HR。
